Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026
Last updated: 29 October 2026
Developer: Margarita Polyakova
App: Rio Flow (iOS and Android)
Bundle / package ID: com.snowingsnake.rioflow

This policy describes what data Rio Flow processes, how it is used, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how you can revoke consent or delete it. It is written to meet Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1 (privacy policy contents, permissions, and third parties) and Google Play data-safety disclosure.

The developer does not operate a user account system and does not receive your lesson progress, microphone audio, or contact details unless you choose to email support.

1. Data processed on your device (not sent to the developer)

The app stores the following locally in an on-device SQLite database and file cache:

  • Learning settings (interface language, topic, pace, difficulty, sound preferences).
  • Lesson progress, phrase history, self-ratings, and spaced-repetition dates.
  • Optional in-app rewards (crystal balance, outfits, skins) — stored only on the device.
  • Prepared lesson speech files cached on the device so lessons can run offline.

How collected: only from your use of the app on that device.
Use: to run lessons, remember progress, and apply your settings.
Sharing: this learning data is not uploaded to the developer and is not sold.

2. Microphone and speech recognition (optional)

Rio Flow requests microphone and speech-recognition permission only for optional lesson features: voice commands (pause, repeat, skip) and pronunciation practice. Lessons work without these permissions. You can refuse the prompt and later change access in system Settings.

Audio is not sent to the developer. Recognition is performed by the operating system (Apple on iOS, the device speech service on Android). Those platforms may process audio according to their own policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

3. Advertising (Google AdMob) — third-party sharing

Store builds may show Google AdMob ads:

  • Interstitial (full-screen) — at most once after the first lesson opened that day.
  • Rewarded — only if you choose to watch an ad in the shop to receive crystals. This is optional and not required to use lessons.

Ads are provided by Google LLC and its advertising partners. To serve, measure, and (where allowed) personalize ads, Google’s SDK may collect or infer identifiers and technical data such as: advertising ID / IDFA (only if you allow tracking on iOS), IP address, device type, OS version, app version, coarse location derived from IP, and ad interaction events (impressions, clicks, reward completion).

Why: to display ads that fund the free app, measure whether an ad was shown or completed, and limit how often ads appear.
Third-party protection: Google’s handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising technologies. We require that this partner protect the data at least as described here.

Where required (including the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), a consent form (Google UMP) is shown before ads are requested. You can change consent later in system or Google ad settings. Paid or core lesson features are not conditioned on accepting tracking or personalized ads.

4. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, if Google’s consent flow requests permission to track, the system App Tracking Transparency prompt appears. Tracking here means linking data from Rio Flow with data from other companies’ apps or websites for advertising or ad measurement, including access to the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).

If you tap “Ask App Not to Track”, Rio Flow still works. Ads may still show in a non-personalized form. The app does not require tracking to use lessons or to receive in-app crystals from a rewarded ad.

5. Notifications and background audio (Android)

On Android, a media-playback notification may appear so a lesson can continue when the screen is off or the app is in the background. This is only for the active lesson. It is not used for marketing, reminders, or newsletters.

6. What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email, or phone number inside the app.
  • No contacts, photos, precise GPS, health, or payment card data.
  • No developer-operated analytics or crash service (no extra analytics SDK of our own).

If you email support, we receive whatever you include in that message (typically device model, OS version, and a description of the issue) solely to reply and fix the problem.

7. Children

Rio Flow is a general-audience language-learning app. It is not directed at children under 13, is not in Apple’s Kids Category, and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through support email, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Retention and deletion

  • On-device learning data: kept until you delete the app or clear the app’s storage. Uninstalling Rio Flow deletes local progress, settings, and the speech cache on that device.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google according to its policies. You can reset your advertising ID in system settings and use Google’s ad settings / consent form to withdraw personalized-ad consent.
  • Support email: kept only as long as needed to handle your request, then deleted from the inbox in the ordinary course of mail handling.

To request deletion of any information you sent by email, write to margaritka251289@gmail.com. We cannot remotely wipe the copy of progress stored only on your phone; uninstall the app (or clear app storage) on that device.

9. How to revoke consent

  • Microphone / speech: iOS Settings → Rio Flow, or Android app permissions.
  • Tracking (iOS): Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ads consent (EEA/UK): the in-app consent form and Google ad settings.
  • Advertising ID: reset in iOS/Android system settings.

10. International users

The developer is based in the jurisdiction from which support email is handled. AdMob may process advertising data on Google servers in other countries. If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you may have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, and objection under GDPR/UK GDPR. Contact the address below. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

11. Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, the “Last updated” date above will change and the new version will be posted at this URL. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
